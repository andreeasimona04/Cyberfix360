About CyberFix360

CyberFix360 is a consultancy-focused cyber security provider. We help small and medium-sized businesses, professional services firms, software companies and public-sector organisations improve their resilience, meet compliance requirements and put security best practice in place.

Services

Comprehensive cybersecurity solutions tailored to protect your organization against evolving digital threats, ensure regulatory compliance, and build resilient infrastructure.

Vulnerability assessments

Systematic identification and cataloging of security flaws across your networks, applications, and endpoints before malicious actors can exploit them.

  • Automated & manual scanning
  • Risk scoring & prioritization
  • Actionable remediation steps

Cyber Essentials certification support

End-to-end guidance through the UK government-backed scheme to protect your organization against common cyber attacks.

  • Readiness self-assessment review
  • Technical control implementation
  • Certification audit preparation

Cyber Essentials Plus preparation

Rigorous hands-on technical verification and testing support to achieve the highest level of Cyber Essentials certification.

  • External vulnerability testing
  • Authenticated workstation audits
  • Simulated phishing & malware defense

ISO 27001 compliance consultancy

  • Gap analysis & risk assessment
  • Policy & procedure drafting
  • Internal audit support

Security architecture reviews

In-depth evaluations of your system design and network topology to embed security best practices from the ground up.

  • Zero-trust framework alignment
  • Network segmentation analysis
  • Threat modeling & mitigation

Penetration testing coordination

Expert management of authorized simulated attacks to test your defenses against real-world threat actors.

  • Post-test remediation tracking

Virtual CISO services

Strategic executive-level cybersecurity leadership and governance without the overhead of a full-time hire.

  • Incident response oversight
